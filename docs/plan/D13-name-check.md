# D13. Name check: Endpaper

**Status:** Findings for the PO. **Not legal clearance.** Source: `docs/research/R1-name-and-competitors.md` (2026-11-07). Items marked [VERIFY] need a primary-source check, most by the PO, because domain and trademark sites were unreachable from this session.

## Summary
"Endpaper" is a good name for this product (the endpaper is the sheet inside a book's cover, where people write their name; it fits "the book of your life"). We found **no journaling app called Endpaper**. Two things need checking before any branding spend:

1. **A recent US trademark application for ENDPAPER in Class 9 (software)**: serial 50121357, filed 2026-09-21, described as document-management/PDF software, not yet examined [VERIFY in USPTO TSDR]. Different product, same word, same broad class. This is the most important item.
2. **Paperblanks' blog "The Endpaper"** (https://blog.paperblanks.com): same word, adjacent audience (people who buy journals). It is a blog, not a product, and we found no Paperblanks software of that name. Risk is confusion and search competition rather than a direct conflict.

Also found: a 2022 registration "E ENDPAPER ENTERTAINMENT" in Class 41 (entertainment) [VERIFY]. Different services.

## Recommendation
Keep **Endpaper** as the working name through wireframes and the beta. Before buying anything beyond a domain (logo, trademark filing, app-store listing), the PO should (a) look up serial 50121357 in USPTO TSDR, (b) do a knock-out search in USPTO, EUIPO and UKIPO for ENDPAPER in Classes 9 and 42, and (c) consider a one-hour consult with a trademark attorney. If the Class 9 application proceeds and the attorney sees a conflict, switch to **Nightleaf** before launch, while switching is still cheap.
